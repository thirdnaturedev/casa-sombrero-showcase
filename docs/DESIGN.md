# Casa Sombrero — Design Direction

## Concept

Casa Sombrero was designed as an immersive digital restaurant environment rather than a conventional scrolling restaurant website.

The experience should feel cinematic, warm, tactile, premium, and distinctly Mexican without becoming visually overcrowded.

## Desktop Principles

- One immersive environment
- Strong central composition
- Cinematic lighting
- Controlled object movement
- Layered depth
- Premium food presentation
- Minimal conventional webpage structure
- Motion used to support discovery

## Visual Language

The project combines:

- warm sunset tones
- dark atmospheric shadows
- aged wood textures
- gold accents
- Mexican architectural references
- premium food photography/rendering
- subtle glow and vignette treatment

## Motion

Animation is designed to feel deliberate rather than constantly active.

Motion includes:

- subtle floating
- parallax
- controlled transitions
- focus changes
- object presentation
- cinematic easing

## Mobile Principles

The mobile ordering system keeps the Casa Sombrero identity while prioritizing usability.

Mobile design focuses on:

- strong readability
- large touch targets
- clear ordering progression
- focused cards
- minimal navigation complexity
- consistent food presentation
